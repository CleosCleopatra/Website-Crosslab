FAQ

Questions we get asked

Does the course cost anything? +

No. Crosslab is free, including materials used in the makerspaces.

Do I need to travel? +

No. You work from your own campus. The two groups connect online during sessions and between them.

How much time does it take? +

One 3-hour evening session per week for 4 weeks, plus about 2 hours per week of contact with your buddy group.

Do I get credits? +

The course does not give academic credits. You receive a certificate of participation.

What language is the course in? +

English.

I study something other than architecture or engineering. Can I apply? +

The course is aimed at architecture and engineering students. If you study something related, email us and explain why you want to join.

Do I need design or prototyping experience? +

No. Mentors at FUSE and UniPod help you with tools and methods.

How are participants selected? +

We look at motivation and aim for a mix of programmes, with an equal number of students from each university.
