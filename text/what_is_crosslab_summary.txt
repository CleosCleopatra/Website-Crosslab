<p>
Crosslab connects students in Sweden, Europe and Rwanda, Africa
to design and build solutions to real-world challenges, from the 
opposite side of the world. </p>
<p>Over four weeks, you'll work with a team to understand a problem
in the other coutnry, brainstorm solutions and bring one of them
to life as a prototype. You'll make things in a real makerspace and 
get real feedback from students int eh other coutnry. </p><p>
Crosslab is free and open to all students. There are no academic 
credicts, but plenty of things to take away. The course will give
you skills in human centered design, international experience and
something for your CV. 
</p>
