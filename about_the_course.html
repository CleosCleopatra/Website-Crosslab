Crosslab Evening Course

About the course

Work together with COUNTRY students to solve a real problem in COUNTRY.

What you will do

Crosslab is a free evening course that runs for one month. You join a small team at your own university and get paired with a buddy group in the other country. Your team designs a solution to a real problem in the other country, under a shared theme.

The course follows human-centered design. You cannot guess what people in the other country need, so instead you get the chance to ask them. Your buddy group will provide you with information, react to your ideas and give feedback on your prototype.

Sessions are hands-on in the makerspaces, FUSE at Chalmers and UniPod at the University Of Rwanda, linked online so both sides can work together.

Duration
4 weeks
Sessions
1 evening/week, 17:30 to 20:00
Location
FUSE (Chalmers) and UniPod (UR)
Cost
Free

What you gain

  • 1

    Cross-cultural competency

    Our world is rapidly growing more and more connected which makes it very important to be able to work with people of other cultures. Rwanda and Sweden are two very different countries with very different cultures, giving you experience in bridging big cultural gaps.

  • 2

    Human-centered design

    You will gain experience interacting with real end-users, practicing how to gather meaningful insight and ask the right questions.

  • 3

    CV

    You will get a certificate that can be put on your CV.

  • 4

    Prototyping

    You will get the opportunity to build a physical prototype, including with tools that you may not otherwise have access to.

  • 5

    International network

    You will make friends halfway across the world, perfect to visit during that long trip you have always wanted to go on.
