Crosslab Evening Course

Tackle a real-world challenge halfway across the world.

Work with students at another university to explore a shared challenge.

What you will do

Crosslab is a free, four-week evening course bringing together students from Chalmers and the University of Rwanda. You will join a small team at your own university and collaborate with a buddy group at the other campus to explore a shared theme and develop a solution together.

Understanding the challenge. Exchange questions, experiences and perspectives with your buddy group to understand the people and context behind the problem.

Turn ideas into prototypes. Combine hands-on work at the FUSE or UniPod makerspace with online collaboration. You will work from your own campus, so no international travel is required.

Duration
4 weeks
Sessions
1 evening/week, 17:30 to 20:00
Location
FUSE (Chalmers) and UniPod (UR)
Cost
Free

What you gain

  • 1

    Cross-cultural collaboration

    Work with students from another culture. Practice communicating, exchanging ideas and solving problems together with students from a very different background.

  • 2

    Human-centered design

    Learn to understand a challenge from the perspective of the people affected by it. Practice asking meaningful questions, gathering insight and using feedback to build a solution.

  • 3

    Certificate for your CV

    Receive a certificate from the Africa Program at Chalmer recognising your participation in the course.

  • 4

    Hands-on prototyping

    Turn your ideas into a prototype and get the chance to work with tools and equipment at your local makerspace that you would otherwise not have access to.

  • 5

    International network

    Get to know students at another university, halfway across the world, building connections far beyond your own campus.
