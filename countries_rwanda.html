Know the place you are designing for

RwandaSweden

Rwanda is a small, hilly, landlocked country in East Africa, often called the land of a thousand hills. It has a young population, a fast-growing tech and innovation scene, and a strong focus on cleanliness and sustainability: plastic bags have been banned since 2008.

Capital

Population

Time zone

Currency
Rwandan franc (RWF)

Good to know when working together

  1. Tip 1

    Greetings matter

    Start meetings with a proper greeting and a few personal words before getting to work. Jumping straight into tasks can feel abrupt.

  2. Tip 2

    Indirect feedback

    Criticism is often expressed politely and indirectly. Ask open questions and give space for honest answers.

  3. Tip 3

    Umuganda

    On the last Saturday of each month, people join community work in the morning. Avoid scheduling then.

  4. Tip 4

    Connectivity

    Mobile data is common, but bandwidth can vary. Share files in light formats and keep video calls flexible.

  5. Tip 5

    Respect and hierarchy

    Titles and elders are respected. Be mindful of tone in group discussions.

  6. Tip 6

    Time and planning

    Plans can shift at short notice. Confirm meeting times a day ahead and agree on a backup channel.
