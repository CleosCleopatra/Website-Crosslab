About us

Two makerspaces, one long collaboration

Crosslab is part of a long-standing collaboration between FUSE makerspace at Chalmers University of Technology and UniPod Makerspace at the University of Rwanda.

As a part of the Africa program at Chalmers, the course brings together students from Rwanda and Sweden to learn and create together.

FUSE

Makerspace at Chalmers, Gothenburg

UniPod

Makerspace at University of Rwanda, Kigali

Chalmers Africa program

A research and education partnerships between Chalmers and the University of Rwanda.
